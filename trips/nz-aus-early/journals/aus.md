Mar 16, 2026  
Next morning stopped in the tiny zoo for breakfast (ate with a Koala).

Morning swim at the the very long pool at our hotel.

Hiked over to the yacht club to see Wild Oats cut in half.  It's a beautiful architectural building with sweeping decks.  Finally got the right motion sickness meds (winds were planned to be 20-30 kts).  

Helicopter ride to Coral Sea Marina was fabulous\!\!\!  Robinson R44 has an awesome, beautiful 270 view in glass\!  Pilot was great (got just briefly worried we were overweight or over size). What an exciting flight.  First around the islands, and then 20 miles out to the Great Barrier Reef.  Awesome, awesome views.

Got on the Eureka (with a 11 passengers).  Great Sydney 60 sailboat is awesome condition.  Crew included Paddy and Brenna (crew).  Mariele, Leia, James, Rachel, Lucy, Skylar, Connor, Joel, and Lara.

We sailed across the straits and anchored between Hooke and Whitsunday island (in a channel).

The boat was great.  Very stable in 25 kt winds.

Mar 17, 2026

Strong winds 20-30kts.  Sailed around the north point of Whitsundays and then down to Whitehaven beach.  Super whites sands and beautiful water color.  

Anchored, ferried shore than too a hike to the viewpoint, and then on to the beach.  Very cool to see stingrays in the very clear water.    Hiked back up to see the view at lower tide.

Sailed downwind (just jib) to the North end of Hooke where there was awesome snorkling.  Really the best I have every seen\!  So healthy.  So many fish.  So many giant clams.

Anchored at the north end of Hooke in a cove.

Stopped at a popular open air dinner joint.  Had seafood.  Headed on into town.
